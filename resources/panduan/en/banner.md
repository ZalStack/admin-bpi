A **banner** is the large image, with a title and short description, shown at the **very top** of a website page. It is the first thing visitors see, so choose a good-quality image.

Open menu: [Banner](cms:admin.banner.index)

## The banner list page

The table shows all banners with these columns:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Order** | Display order (smaller numbers come first) |
| **Page** | The website page where the banner appears |
| **Title** | Banner title (default language) |
| **Image** | Small preview image |
| **Status** | Active = shown, Inactive = hidden. Click to change. |
| **Action** | ✏️ Edit, 🗑️ Delete |

## Adding a banner

1. Click **Add Banner**.
2. Choose the **Page** where the banner will appear:
   - 🏠 **Home**: the home page
   - 👥 **Stakeholders**: the Stakeholders page
   - 🎬 **Projects**: the Projects page
   - 📰 **Articles**: the News page
   - 🏛️ **About Us**: the About Us page
   - 📞 **Contact Us**: the Contact page
3. Fill in **Order**, for example `1`.
4. Leave **Status: Active** ticked if the banner should appear right away.
5. In the **ID** tab, fill in **Title** and **Description**. Both are required.
6. Switch to the **EN** tab and fill in the English title and description.
7. Under **Image**, click and choose an image (JPG, PNG, WEBP; maximum 2 MB).
8. Click **Save**.

## Banners marked "(Legacy)"

Some old banners, for example for the **Strategic Programs** and **Partners** pages, are marked **⚠️ … (Legacy)** in the **Page** field when edited. Those pages have been merged into other pages on the website (Programs into **Projects**, Partners into **Stakeholders**), so these banners are probably **no longer shown**. To change the banner of the Projects or Stakeholders page, edit the banner whose Page is **Projects** or **Stakeholders**. Ask the technical team before deleting a Legacy banner.

## Changing a banner

1. Click the ✏️ icon on the banner you want to change.
2. Change what you need. To replace the image, just choose a new file. The old image is replaced automatically.
3. Click **Update**.

## Tips for a good banner

- Use a **landscape (wide)** image at least **1920 pixels** wide, so it stays sharp on large screens.
- Put important things (faces, logos) in the **center**, because the left and right sides can be cut off on phones.
- The title is written on top of the image, so avoid images that are too busy or too bright, so the text stays readable.
- Keep the title **short** (ideally fewer than 8 words) and the description to 1–2 sentences.

> **Tip:** For temporary banners (for example an event promotion), set the status to **Inactive** after the event ends. Do not delete it, so you can reuse it next year.

> **Important:** Try to keep **one active banner** per page. If a page has several active banners, the **Order** field decides which one comes first.
