The most common problems and how to solve them. If your problem is not listed, see [Contacting the technical team](#contacting-the-technical-team) at the end of this chapter.

## Logging in and accounts

**I cannot log in and see "Incorrect email or password".**
Check the spelling of your email, make sure **Caps Lock** is off, and click the eye icon to see the password you typed. If it still fails, ask the Super Admin to reset your password.

**I tried many times and now I cannot log in at all.**
For security, logins are limited to 5 attempts per minute. Wait about 1 minute, then try again carefully.

**I was suddenly logged out and lost what I typed in a form.**
Your session ends automatically if the CMS is not used for about 2 hours. Log in again and fill in the form again. For long texts, write them in Word/Google Docs first, or save regularly.

**I see a "403" or "no access" page.**
That page is not available for your role (for example, an Editor opening the Banner menu). If you really need access, contact the Super Admin.

**I see "419 Page Expired" when pressing save.**
The page was left open too long. Reload the page (F5), log in if asked, then fill in the form again and save.

## Saving data

**I pressed save, but the data was not saved and a red box appeared.**
Something is missing or wrong. Read the list of errors in the red box (explained in [Basics](bab:dasar#2-required-fields-and-error-messages)). Also check for language tabs with a **red dot**.

**After a failed save, the image I chose is gone.**
This is normal. For security, browsers do not keep the chosen file. Choose the image again and save.

**My image was rejected.**
The file is probably larger than 2 MB or in an unsupported format (for example SVG, HEIC from an iPhone, or PDF). Convert it to JPG/PNG and make it smaller. See [Basics](bab:dasar#5-uploading-images).

**I deleted something by accident. Can it be restored?**
Deleted data cannot be restored from the CMS. Contact the technical team right away. They may be able to restore it from a database *backup*, if one exists. In future, use the **Inactive** or **Draft** status instead of deleting.

## What the website shows

**I saved, but the website has not changed.**
1. Make sure the status is **Active** or **Published**.
2. Reload the website page with **Ctrl + F5**, or open it in a private (*Incognito*) window.
3. Make sure you are looking at the right **language** (ID or EN).
4. If it still has not changed after a few minutes, contact the technical team.

**The English website still shows Indonesian text.**
The **EN** tab was empty when it was saved, so it was filled with a copy of the Indonesian text. Open that item in the CMS, replace the **EN** tab content with an English translation, and save. See [Basics](bab:dasar#1-two-languages-language-tabs).

**My article does not appear on the website.**
Check its status in the News menu. It must be **Published**, not Draft or Archived.

**A partner logo does not appear.**
Check that both the partner and its **partner category** are **Active**, and that the logo has been uploaded.

**A board member appears in the wrong department box, or one department is split into two boxes.**
The **Department / Unit** names of the members are not exactly the same. See the [Org. Structure](bab:struktur#how-members-are-grouped) chapter.

**A news category's colored label has disappeared.**
The category title was probably changed. Edit the article, choose its category again in each language tab, and save. See the [News](bab:berita#news-categories) chapter.

**A shortlink or QR code opens a "not found" page.**
Check that the shortlink is **Active** and that its **Short Code** was not changed. See the [Shortlink](bab:shortlink) chapter.

## Contacting the technical team

Contact the technical team or developer when:

- An error appears that this guide does not explain (for example a "500 Server Error" page).
- Data was deleted and needs to be restored.
- A new feature, page, or language is needed on the website.
- Contact message notification emails never arrive.

**To get your problem solved quickly, include:**

1. The menu and page you were on. Also copy the address from the browser's address bar.
2. What you were doing, for example "saving a new article with a 1.5 MB image".
3. A **screenshot** of the error message. On Windows, press **Windows + Shift + S**.
4. When it happened (date and time).
