The **Partners** menu manages BPI's **partner organizations** and their logos. Partner logos appear on the home page (the *Mitra Kami* / Our Partners section) and on the **Stakeholders** page (the *Mitra Strategis* / Strategic Partners tab), grouped by category.

Open menu: [Partners](cms:admin.mitra.index)

This menu has three parts:

| Part | Button on the Partners page | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **Partner list** | (main page) | Each partner's name, logo, category, and website |
| **Partner Categories** | **Manage Partner Categories** | Partner groups, for example Strategic, International, Industry |
| **Partner Page Intro** | **Partner Page Intro** | The heading and description that introduce the partners section on the Stakeholders page |

## First step: set up partner categories

Every partner **must** belong to one category, so make sure the category exists before adding a partner.

1. On the Partners page, click **Manage Partner Categories**.
2. Click **Add Category**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Slug / Category Code**: a short code without spaces, for example `strategis` or `internasional`. You can leave it empty to generate it from the category name.
   - **Display Order**: the order of the group on the website.
   - **Status: Active**: tick it so the category can be chosen.
   - **Category Name** in the **ID** and **EN** tabs, for example *Mitra Strategis* / *Strategic Partner*.
4. Click **Save Category**.

> **Warning:** Partners are linked to their category through the **slug** (category code). Do not change the slug of a category that is already in use. If you do, the partners in that category lose their group and must be edited one by one. Changing the category **name** is safe.

Categories with **Inactive** status are not offered when adding or editing a partner.

## Adding a partner

1. On the Partners page, click **Add Partner**.
2. Choose the **Partner Category**.
3. (Optional) Fill in the partner's **Website URL**, including `https://`, for example `https://example-partner.com`.
4. Fill in **Display Order** (the logo's order within its category) and tick **Display Status: Active**.
5. In the **ID** tab, fill in **Partner Name**. Fill it in the **EN** tab too (usually the same).
6. Under **Partner Logo**, choose the logo file.
7. Click **Save Partner**.

> **Tip:** Use a **PNG logo with a transparent background** so it blends with the website background. Crop the empty space around the logo so all logos look the same size. Logos are automatically shrunk to at most 600 pixels.

On the list page, partners are sorted by **category**, then **order**, then **name**.

## Changing or hiding a partner

- **Change:** click ✏️, change the data, then click **Update Partner**. To replace the logo, just choose a new file.
- **Hide:** click the **Active** label until it changes to **Inactive**. The logo will no longer appear on the website.

> **Important:** Partners can also be chosen as **collaborators** in the [Projects](bab:proyek) menu. If a partner is deleted, it also disappears from the collaborator list of every project. If the partnership is only paused, use the **Inactive** status.

## Partner Page Intro (the partners section introduction)

This sets the **heading and description** above the partner logos on the Stakeholders page.

1. On the Partners page, click **Partner Page Intro**.
2. In each language tab, fill in:
   - **Section Title (Badge)**: a small label, for example *Mitra Kami* / *Our Partners*.
   - **Subheadline / Main Headline**: the large heading, for example *Kolaborasi Membangun Masa Depan Perfilman Indonesia*.
   - **Full Description**: the explanation paragraph.
3. Tick **Display Status**. If it is not ticked, the website shows its built-in heading text.
4. Click **Save Intro Settings**.

> **Note:** There is an **Intro Photo / Image** field, but the current website design **does not show** this photo.
