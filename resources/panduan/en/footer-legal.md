The **Footer & Legal** menu manages three things:

1. **Footer**: the texts at the very bottom of every website page.
2. **Privacy Policy**: the Privacy Policy page.
3. **Terms & Conditions**: the Terms & Conditions page.

Open menu: [Footer & Legal](cms:admin.footer.texts)

At the top of the page there are three tab buttons: **Footer**, **Privacy Policy**, and **Terms & Conditions**. Click a tab to switch pages.

Changing the texts in all three tabs works like Page Texts: change the **ID** and **EN** fields, then click **Save Texts**. An empty field goes back to the built-in text. See the [Page Texts](bab:teks-halaman) chapter for details.

## The Footer tab

| Group | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Brand Column** | **Tagline**: a short sentence below the BPI logo |
| **Column: About BPI** | The column heading and the link names Profile, Vision, Mission, Organizational Structure |
| **Column: Information** | The column heading and the link names Programs & Roadmap, Collaborative Projects, News, Opinion |
| **Column: Network & Contact** | The column heading and the link names Stakeholders, Contact Us |
| **Bottom Bar** | The copyright text (the year is added automatically) and the link names Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions |

> **Note:** The **address, email, and social media links** in the footer come automatically from the [Contact](bab:kontak) menu. Change them in the Contact menu, not here.

## The Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions tabs

Both legal pages have the same fields:

| Group | Field | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Page Header** | **Badge** | A small label above the heading |
| | **Headline** | The opening sentence of the page |
| | **Last Updated Date** | When it was last updated, for example *7 September 2026* |
| **Content** | **Page Content** | The full document, written with the text editor |

**To update a document:**

1. Click the **Privacy Policy** or **Terms & Conditions** tab.
2. Change **Page Content** in the **ID · Bahasa Indonesia** and **EN · English** fields. Use **Heading 2** for each article heading, for example *1. Introduction*.
3. Update the **Last Updated Date** in both languages.
4. Click **Save Texts**.

> **Important:** The Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions are **legal documents**. Only change them based on a text **approved** by BPI's leadership or legal team, and always update the **Last Updated Date** whenever the content changes.

> **Tip:** If the text was written in Word, copy it section by section and paste with **Ctrl + Shift + V** so Word's formatting is not carried over. Then set the article headings and numbered lists again with the editor buttons.
