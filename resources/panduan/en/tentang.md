The **About** menu manages the **About Us** page: the BPI profile, the Vision, and the Mission. The profile summary also appears on the home page.

Open menu: [About](cms:admin.tentang.index)

## The three sections of the About page

| Section in the CMS | What it shows on the website |
|---|---|
| **Introduction / Profile** (intro) | A small label, the main headline, the BPI profile text, and a photo |
| **BPI Vision** (visi) | The vision heading and text, with certain words highlighted |
| **BPI Mission** (misi) | A heading, an introduction sentence, and the mission cards |

Each section only needs to exist **once**. To change its content, click the ✏️ icon (**Edit Content & Points**) on that section.

Every section has these fields:

- **Display Order (Position)**: the order of the section on the page.
- **Display Status: Show on Landing Page**: tick it to show the section on the website.

## Changing the profile (Introduction / Profile)

1. Click ✏️ on the **Introduction / Profile** row.
2. In the **ID** tab, fill in:
   - **Section Title (Badge)**: the small label above the heading, for example *Tentang Kami*.
   - **Subtitle / Main Headline**: the large heading, for example *Membangun Masa Depan Perfilman Nasional*.
   - **Full Description**: the BPI profile paragraph.
3. Repeat for the **EN** tab.
4. (Optional) Under **Section Photo / Image**, choose a new photo to replace this section's photo.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

## Changing the Vision

1. Click ✏️ on the **BPI Vision** row.
2. In each language tab, fill in:
   - **Vision Title**: for example *Visi Kami* / *Our Vision*.
   - **Vision Statement**: the full vision statement.
   - **Highlighted Words / Phrases**: words or phrases from the vision text that should be **highlighted** (shown bold on a colored background) on the website. Separate them with commas.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

**Example:** In the picture below, the highlight field contains `unggul dan kompetitif, kompeten, sehat dan kondusif, sehat`. On the website (bottom of the picture), these four phrases are highlighted in the vision text.

![Top: the highlighted words field in the CMS. Bottom: the result on the About Us page, with those phrases highlighted](/images/panduan/visi-highlight.webp)

> **Important:** The highlighted words must be **exactly the same** as in the vision text, including spelling. Fill in the highlight field in **each language**, because the words differ between the Indonesian and English versions.

## Changing the Mission and its cards

1. Click ✏️ on the **BPI Mission** row.
2. In the language tabs, fill in **Mission Title** and **Mission Introduction** (the introduction sentence before the mission cards).
3. Scroll down to **Mission Card Points**. Each card has:
   - **Point Icon**: click **Select** to choose an icon.
   - **Order**: the card order.
   - **Point Status: Active**: tick it to show the card.
   - **Point Title** and **Point Description** for each language.
4. To add a card, click **Add Point**. To remove a card, click **Delete** on that card.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

> **Warning:** Cards removed with the **Delete** button are only really deleted after you press **Save Changes**. If you removed a card by mistake, click **Cancel** (do not save) and open the form again.

## Another button on the About page

The **Structure Section Texts** button on the About list page changes the headings and labels of the **Organizational Structure** section on the About page. See the [Page Texts](bab:teks-halaman) chapter. The board members themselves are managed in the [Org. Structure](bab:struktur) menu.
