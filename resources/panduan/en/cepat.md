Not sure which menu to open? Find your task in the tables below. The **Open menu** links open the related CMS page in a new tab.

## Daily tasks

| I want to… | Open menu | Then… | Chapter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Publish a news article | [News](cms:admin.berita.index) | **Add News**, fill it in, choose **Published**, save | [News](bab:berita) |
| Hide an old article | [News](cms:admin.berita.index) | Click the **Published** label until it changes to **Draft** | [News](bab:berita) |
| Add event photos to an article | [News](cms:admin.berita.index) | Click the gallery icon (four gold squares), then **Add Gallery** | [News](bab:berita#news-photo-gallery) |
| Read messages from visitors | [Contact Messages](cms:admin.kontak-form.index) | Click the eye icon on a message | [Contact Messages](bab:pesan-kontak) |
| Change the large image at the top of a page | [Banner](cms:admin.banner.index) | Edit that page's banner and choose a new image | [Banner](bab:banner) |
| Create a short link to share | [Shortlink](cms:admin.shortlink.index) | **Add Shortlink** | [Shortlink](bab:shortlink) |
| Create a QR code for a poster/banner | [QR Code](cms:admin.qrcode.index) | **Add QR Code**, then download PNG/SVG | [QR Code](bab:qrcode) |

## Changing page content

| I want to… | Open menu | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| Change the "About Us" text | [About](cms:admin.tentang.index) → edit **Introduction / Profile** | [About](bab:tentang) |

| Add, change, or remove a board member | [Org. Structure](cms:admin.struktur.index) | [Org. Structure](bab:struktur) |
| Arrange the department boxes in the org chart | [Org. Structure](cms:admin.struktur.index) → **Chart Layout** | [Org. Structure](bab:struktur#arranging-the-org-chart-chart-layout) |
| Add a new partner logo | [Partners](cms:admin.mitra.index) → **Add Partner** | [Partners](bab:mitra) |
| Add a professional association | [Stakeholder](cms:admin.stakeholder.index) → **Add Stakeholder** | [Stakeholders](bab:stakeholder) |
| Add a collaborative project | [Projects](cms:admin.proyek.index) → **Add Project** | [Projects](bab:proyek) |
| Change strategic programs or the roadmap | [Programs](cms:admin.program.index) | [Programs & Roadmap](bab:program) |
| Reorder or hide sections on the homepage | [Homepage](cms:admin.beranda.index) | [Homepage](bab:beranda) |
| Change the office address, email, WhatsApp, or social media | [Contact](cms:admin.kontak.index) | [Contact](bab:kontak) |

| Change the Privacy Policy or Terms & Conditions | [Footer & Legal](cms:admin.footer.texts) | [Footer & Legal](bab:footer-legal) |
| Change the text at the very bottom of the website | [Footer & Legal](cms:admin.footer.texts) | [Footer & Legal](bab:footer-legal) |
| Add or change a menu item at the top of the website | [Menu](cms:admin.menu.index) | [Navigation Menu](bab:menu) |
