The **News** menu is used to write and publish **news/articles** on the website's News page. The latest news also appears on the home page. Both **Admins** and **Editors** can use this menu.

Open menu: [News](cms:admin.berita.index)

The News menu has four parts:

| Part | How to open it | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **News list** | The **News** menu | All articles and their status |
| **News Categories** | The **Manage News Categories** button | News categories and their label colors |
| **News Tags** | The **Manage News Tags** button | Tags/topics for grouping articles |
| **News Gallery** | The gallery icon (four small gold squares) on an article | Extra photos inside an article |

## Before writing: set up categories and tags

Every article **must** have a category and can have several tags. Check first that the categories and tags you need exist. If not, add them first (see [News categories](#news-categories) and [News tags](#news-tags) below).

**Categories vs. tags:**

- A **category** is the main type of article. An article has only **one** category, for example *Festival*, *Industri* (industry), or *Opini* (opinion). The category appears as a colored label on the news card.
- A **tag** is an extra topic or keyword. An article can have **several** tags, for example *#FFI2026*, *#FilmDaerah*, or *#Regulasi*.

## Writing and publishing an article

1. Click **Add News**.
2. **Part 1 – General Information**
   - **Author** (required): the writer's name.
   - **Publication Date** (required): the article date. It is filled in with today's date automatically and can be changed.
   - **Publication Status**: **Published** (shown right away), **Draft** (saved for later), or **Archived**.
   - **Main Image / News Cover**: the cover image (landscape 16:9, maximum 2 MB).
3. **Part 2 – News Tags & Topics**: tick the relevant tags. Only **Active** tags appear here.
4. **Part 3 – Multilingual Article Content**: fill in the **ID** tab, then repeat in the **EN** tab:
   - **News Title** (required): the article title.
   - **Category** (required): choose a category.
   - **Short Summary (Excerpt)** (required): a 1–2 sentence summary shown on the news card.
   - **Full News Content** (required): the full article, written with the text editor (see [Basics](bab:dasar#7-the-text-editor-rich-text-editor)).
   - **Key Quotes / Highlight Quote** (optional): an important quote to highlight.
5. Click **Save News**.

> **Warning:** The default status of a new article is **Published**. This means the article **appears on the website immediately** when saved. If the article is not ready or still needs to be checked by a supervisor, change **Publication Status** to **Draft** before pressing **Save News**.

> **Important:** The **Category** is chosen **in each language tab**. After choosing a category in the ID tab (for example *Festival*), choose the same category in the EN tab too (its English version).

> **Note:** The **Publication Date** is only the date that is displayed and used to sort articles (newest first). A future date does **not** make the article go live automatically on that date. The article appears as soon as its status is **Published**.

### Checklist before publishing

- [ ] The title and summary are filled in in **both Indonesian and English**
- [ ] A category is chosen in **both** language tabs
- [ ] A cover image is uploaded and is not blurry
- [ ] Names of people, positions, and organizations are spelled correctly
- [ ] The relevant tags are ticked
- [ ] The status is correct (**Published** if it should be shown)
- [ ] After saving, the article has been checked on the website

## Changing, hiding, or deleting an article

- **Change:** click ✏️ on the article, change the content, then click **Update News**.
- **Hide for a while:** on the list page, click the **Published** label until it changes to **Draft**. Click again to publish it again.
- **Archive:** open Edit, change **Publication Status** to **Archived**, then save.
- **Delete:** click 🗑️ and confirm.

> **Warning:** Deleting an article also permanently deletes **all of its gallery photos**. Consider using **Draft** or **Archived** instead.

## News photo gallery

The gallery holds supporting photos, such as event documentation, shown inside the article.

1. Open the gallery in one of these ways:
   - On the news list page, click the **gallery icon** (four small gold squares, to the left of the pencil icon) on the article, or
   - In the article's Edit form, click the **Manage Gallery** button in the top right.
2. Click **Add Gallery**.
3. Choose an **Image** (required; JPG, PNG, GIF, or WEBP, maximum 2 MB).
4. (Optional) Fill in the photo's **Title** and **Description** in each language tab.
5. Fill in **Order** and tick **Status: Active**.
6. Click **Save**. Repeat for each photo.

On the gallery page, click a photo to enlarge it. Use ✏️ / 🗑️ to change or delete a photo, and click the **Active/Inactive** label to hide a particular photo.

> **Tip:** Photos must be added **one by one**. Pick the best photos first (5–10 photos is enough) and make them smaller before uploading.

## News categories

Open it with the **Manage News Categories** button on the News page.

### Adding a category

1. Click **Add Category**.
2. In each language tab, fill in:
   - **Category Title**: for example *Festival* / *Festival*.
   - **Slug**: can be left empty; it is generated from the title.
3. Under **Category Badge Color**, choose the label color. Click one of the **Color Presets** (for example **Maroon (BPI)**, **Navy Blue (BPI)**, or **Gold (BPI)**), or type your own color code.
4. Check the **Live Preview Badge** to see how the label will look on the news card.
5. Click **Save Category**.

> **Warning:** Articles are linked to their category through the **category title**. If you change a category title (for example *Industri* to *Industri Film*), older articles still use the old title, so their label color may disappear. After changing a category title, open and re-save the older articles, choosing the new category. That is why you should **avoid renaming categories** that are already used a lot.

## News tags

Open it with the **Manage News Tags** button on the News page.

1. Click **Add Tag**.
2. Fill in **Tag** in each language tab, for example *Festival Film Indonesia*.
3. **Slug** can be left empty.
4. Tick **Status: Active** so the tag can be chosen when writing an article.
5. Click **Save**.

Tags set to **Inactive** are no longer offered when writing an article.

## Tips for writing good news

- **Title**: clear and informative, ideally 6–12 words.
- **Summary**: answer "what, who, when, where" in 1–2 sentences.
- **Body**: use short paragraphs (3–4 sentences) and sub-headings (Heading 2) for long articles.
- **Cover image**: choose the photo that best represents the article, in landscape format.
- **Links**: when mentioning another website, add a link with the 🔗 button in the editor.
