The **Role Management** menu is only available to the **Super Admin**. It is used to create CMS accounts for BPI staff, set their role (**Admin** or **Editor**), reset passwords, and delete accounts.

Open menu: [Role Management](cms:admin.role-management.index)

## The account list page

The top shows the number of accounts per role. Below that is a short explanation of the **Admin** role (manages all website content) and the **Editor** role (manages news only). The account table shows the name, email, role, and creation date. Your own account is marked **(You)**.

**Super Admin** accounts are marked **Protected** and cannot be changed or deleted from this menu.

## Adding an account

1. Click **Add User**.
2. Fill in **Name**: the staff member's full name.
3. Fill in **Email**: the email they will use to log in.
4. Choose a **Role**:
   - **Admin**: for staff who manage all website content.
   - **Editor**: for staff who only write news.
5. Fill in **Password** (at least 8 characters) and repeat it in **Confirm Password**.
6. Click **Save User**.
7. Give the **email and password** to the staff member in person or by private message, and ask them to change the password right away in the **Profile** menu.

> **Tip:** Give each person only the role they need. Staff who only write news should get the **Editor** role, so they cannot change other parts of the website by accident.

## Changing a role or resetting a password

1. Click ✏️ on the account.
2. Change **Name**, **Email**, or **Role** as needed.
3. To **reset the password** (for example when the staff member forgot it), fill in **Password** and **Confirm Password** with a new password. If you do not want to change the password, **leave both fields empty**.
4. Click **Update User**.

## Deleting an account

When a staff member no longer works on the website, delete their account with the 🗑️ icon and confirm. Content they created (news, projects, and so on) is **not** deleted.

## Safe habits

- **One person, one account.** Do not create a shared account, so every change can be traced to a person.
- **Delete accounts right away** for staff who have left or moved to another division.
- **Review the account list** regularly, for example every 3 months.
- Do not share passwords in group chats or emails read by many people.
