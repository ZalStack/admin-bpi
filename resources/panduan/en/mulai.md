Welcome! This chapter explains what the CMS is, how to log in and out, and how to read the CMS screen. Please read this chapter and the [Basics](bab:dasar) chapter before you start changing website content.

## What is the CMS?

The **CMS** (*Content Management System*) is the "control room" of the Badan Perfilman Indonesia (BPI) website. Everything shown on the website, such as texts, images, news, partner logos, and the organizational structure, **comes from this CMS**.

Think of the website as a **shop window**, and the CMS as the **staff room behind it**. Visitors only see the shop window. You arrange what is in the window from the staff room. So when you change something in the CMS and save it, the website changes too, without needing a programmer.

> **Note:** After saving, open the related website page and reload it (press **F5**, or **Ctrl + F5** if the change does not show yet). If the change still does not appear after a few minutes, contact the technical team.

## Logging in

1. Open the CMS address in a browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox). The Super Admin or technical team will give you the CMS address.
2. Fill in **Email Address** and **Password**.
3. Tick **Remember Me** *only* on your own computer. Do not tick it on a shared or public computer.
4. Click **Log In to Dashboard**.

If the message **"Incorrect email or password. Please try again."** appears, check the spelling of your email and password. Mind upper and lower case letters, and make sure **Caps Lock** is off. Click the eye icon in the password field to see what you typed.

> **Important:** For security, the CMS only allows **5 login attempts per minute**. If you get it wrong too often, wait about 1 minute before trying again.

**Forgot your password?** The CMS has no "forgot password" button. Contact the BPI **Super Admin**, who can set a new password for you (see the [Role Management](bab:role) chapter). Once you can log in, change that password right away in the **Profile** menu (see the [Profile & Account Security](bab:akun) chapter).

## Logging out

There are two ways:

- Click **Logout** at the very bottom of the left menu (sidebar), or
- Click the circle with your initials in the top-right corner, then choose **Logout**.

Always log out when you use a computer that other people also use.

> **Important:** If the CMS is left idle for about **2 hours**, you are logged out automatically. Anything typed in a form but not saved yet will be **lost**. When writing long texts (such as news), save regularly, or write in Word/Google Docs first and then copy it into the CMS.

## The CMS screen

After logging in, the CMS screen has three parts. The numbers in the picture below match the table.

![The CMS after logging in: (1) top bar, (2) sidebar, (3) work area, (4) the Page Guide button](/images/panduan/tampilan-cms.webp)

| Part | Where | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **Top bar** | Very top, maroon | Shows the BPI logo and your name. On phones/tablets it has a ☰ button to open the menu. |
| **Sidebar (left menu)** | Left side, dark blue | All the menus you are allowed to open. The open menu is marked with a gold line. |
| **Work area** | Center | Where you view, add, and change data. |

In the bottom-right corner of most pages there is a **❓ Page Guide** button. Click it to open the guide chapter that explains the page you are on.

### Every menu follows the same pattern

Almost every menu works the same way. Once you understand one menu, the others are easy. Each menu opens with a **list page**: a table with all the data (for example, all banners). This page has four important buttons:

![The Banner list page: (1) Add Banner button, (2) edit icon, (3) delete icon, (4) status label](/images/panduan/pola-halaman-daftar.webp)

1. The **Add …** button (for example **Add Banner**) in the top right, to **add** new data.
2. The **pencil** icon (✏️) in the *Action* column, to **change** (edit) data.
3. The **trash** icon (🗑️) in the *Action* column, to **delete** data.
4. The **Active / Inactive** label in the *Status* column. Click it to show or hide the data on the website.

These four are explained in detail in the [Basics](bab:dasar) chapter.

## Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is the first page after logging in. It contains:

- **Number boxes**: how many Banners, Homepage sections, Stakeholders, Programs, Projects, and active languages there are. Click **View All** to open the related menu.
- **Latest Banners / Latest Homepages / Latest Projects**: the most recently added data.
- **Quick Actions**: shortcuts to add a Banner, a Homepage section, or a Project, or to open the language settings.
- **System Information**: technical information for the IT team. You can ignore it.

> **Note:** If you log in as an **Editor**, some Dashboard buttons lead to menus an Editor cannot open. Clicking them shows a "403" or "no access" page. This is normal. Editors only need the **News** menu.

## Access rights (roles)

Each account has one **role** that decides which menus it can open:

| Role | Who | Can manage |
|---|---|---|
| **Super Admin** | Main administrator / technical team | Every menu, including **Role Management** (accounts) and **API Documentation**. |
| **Admin** | BPI website staff | All website content: Banner, Homepage, About, Partners, Stakeholder, Programs, Projects, News, Org. Structure, Contact, Contact Messages, Menu, Footer & Legal, Language Settings, Shortlink, and QR Code. |
| **Editor** | News writers / editorial team | Only **News**: articles, news categories, tags, and news galleries. |

Your role is shown on the small card at the bottom of the sidebar, under your name. If you need more access, contact the Super Admin.
