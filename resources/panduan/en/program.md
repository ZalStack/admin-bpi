The **Programs** menu manages two things shown on the **Projects** page (the *Program & Peta Jalan* / Programs & Roadmap tab) and on the home page:

1. **Strategic Program Pillars**: BPI's strategic program pillars, each with several **sub-points**.
2. **4-Year Roadmap**: the year-by-year roadmap for transforming the film industry.

Open menu: [Programs](cms:admin.program.index)

The Programs page shows these two tables, one above the other.

## Part 1: Program pillars

### Adding a program pillar

1. Click **Add Program**.
2. Under **Program Pillar Icon**, click **Select** to choose an icon.
3. Fill in **Order** and tick **Status: Active**.
4. In the **ID** tab, fill in:
   - **Program Pillar Title**: the pillar name, for example *Pembiayaan dan Investasi* (Financing and Investment).
   - **Introduction Description**: a short explanation of the pillar.
5. Repeat for the **EN** tab.
6. In **Sub-Program Points List**, click **Add Sub-Point** for each activity or focus area in this pillar. Each sub-point has:
   - **Sub-Point Icon**: click **Select** to choose an icon.
   - **Order** and active status.
   - **Sub-Point Title** for each language, for example *Penguatan Skema Insentif* (Strengthening the Incentive Scheme).
7. Click **Save Program**.

### Changing a program pillar and its sub-points

1. Click ✏️ (**Edit Program & Points**) on the pillar.
2. Change what you need. The Edit form also has an **Image / Thumbnail** field (optional) for the pillar image.
3. To add a sub-point, click **Add Sub-Point**. To remove one, click **Delete Point** on that sub-point.
4. Click **Update Program**.

> **Warning:** Deleting a **program pillar** from the list page also deletes **all of its sub-points**. Sub-points removed in the Edit form are only really deleted after you press **Update Program**.

## Part 2: Roadmap

Each roadmap row stands for one year or one period, for example *2025* or *2025 – 2026*.

### Adding a roadmap entry

1. In the **4-Year Roadmap** table, click **Add Roadmap**.
2. Fill in **Roadmap Year**, for example `2026`.
3. (Optional) Choose a **Roadmap Icon**.
4. Fill in **Order** and tick **Status: Active**.
5. In the **ID** tab, fill in:
   - **Roadmap Theme / Title**: the theme of that year, for example *Fondasi & Konsolidasi* (Foundation & Consolidation).
   - **Roadmap Description**: what that year focuses on.
   - **Achievement Points**: a list of targets or achievements. Click **Add Point** to add a row and the 🗑️ icon to remove one.
6. Repeat for the **EN** tab. Achievement points are filled in separately for each language. If the EN achievement points are left empty, the points from the ID tab are copied.
7. (Optional) Choose a **Roadmap Logo / Image**. If set, this image is shown instead of the icon.
8. Click **Save Roadmap**.

To change or delete a roadmap entry, use the ✏️ or 🗑️ icon on that row.

> **Tip:** Order the roadmap by year (Order 1 for the earliest year) so it reads from left to right in sequence.

## Texts on the Projects page

Headings such as "Program Strategis BPI" and "Tahapan Transformasi Perfilman", and the statistics at the top of the page, are changed with the **Projects Page Texts** button. See the [Page Texts](bab:teks-halaman) chapter.
