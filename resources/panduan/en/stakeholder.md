The **Stakeholder** menu manages the **professional associations** and film industry stakeholders connected to BPI. They appear on the **Stakeholders** page (the *Asosiasi Profesi* / Professional Associations tab).

Open menu: [Stakeholder](cms:admin.stakeholder.index)

## Adding a stakeholder

1. Click **Add Stakeholder**.
2. (Optional) Under **Icon Stakeholder**, click **Select** to choose an icon. The icon is used when the stakeholder has no logo.
3. Fill in **Order** and tick **Status: Active**.
4. In the **ID** tab, fill in:
   - **Name**: the association name, for example *Asosiasi Produser Film Indonesia (APROFI)*.
   - **Description**: a short explanation of the association.
5. Repeat in the **EN** tab.
6. Under **Logo / Association Image**, choose the association's logo.
7. Click **Save**.

> **Note:** If a logo is uploaded, the website shows the **logo**, not the icon. Logos are automatically shrunk to at most 800 pixels. Use a square PNG logo for a tidy look.

## Changing, hiding, or deleting

- **Change:** click ✏️, change the data, then click **Update**.
- **Hide:** click the **Active** label until it changes to **Inactive**.
- **Delete:** click 🗑️ and confirm. The logo is deleted too.

## Texts on the Stakeholders page

The page heading, the statistics ("Asosiasi Profesi Terdaftar"), the tab names, and the call to action "Daftarkan Asosiasi Anda" are changed with the **Page Texts** button in the top right of this page. See the [Page Texts](bab:teks-halaman) chapter.

The **partners** that also appear on the Stakeholders page are managed in the [Partners](bab:mitra) menu.
