The **Contact** menu manages all BPI contact information shown on the website's **Contact** page: office address, Google Maps location, social media, email, and phone/WhatsApp numbers. Some of it (address, email, and social media) is also used in the **footer** (the very bottom) of every page.

Open menu: [Contact](cms:admin.kontak.index)

> **Note:** Usually there is only **one** contact record. To change the contact information, **edit the existing record** (✏️ icon). Do not create a new contact record.

## Changing the contact information

Click ✏️ on the contact record. The form has five parts.

### 1. Location & Map (Google Maps)

1. Open **Google Maps** and find the BPI office.
2. Click **Share**, then **Copy link** (the address usually starts with `https://maps.app.goo.gl/...`).
3. Paste the link into the **Google Maps Link** field.

The system automatically reads the coordinates from the link and connects the "Open in Google Maps" button on the website. You only need **Manual Coordinate Settings (Latitude / Longitude)** if the map points to the wrong place.

**Status: Active** must stay ticked for the contact information to appear on the website.

### 2. Social Media

The list of BPI's official social media accounts. Click **+ Add Social Media Account** to add a row, then fill in:

- **Platform**: Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, Twitter / X, or Website.
- **Username / Handle**: for example `@bpi.indonesia`.
- **Profile URL**: can be left empty; it is generated from the username. Only fill it in if the profile address does not follow the usual pattern.

Click the 🗑️ icon to remove a row.

### 3. Email Address

Click **+ Add Email** to add an official email address, for example `info@bpi.or.id`. The **Mailto URL** field can be left empty.

> **Important:** The **first email** in the list is used as the main email, for example for the "Contact the Secretariat" buttons on the Projects and Stakeholders pages. Put the most actively used email at the top.

### 4. Phone Number & WhatsApp

Click **+ Add Number**, then fill in:

- **Contact Number**: the phone number, for example `0878 3992 0990` or `+62 878 3992 0990`.
- **Type**: **WhatsApp**, **Office Phone**, **Hotline**, or **Fax**.
- **Click-to-Action URL**: can be left empty. For WhatsApp, the system builds the `https://wa.me/62…` link automatically (a leading 0 is changed to 62).

### 5. Office & Contact Page Information (two languages)

Fill in each language tab:

| Group | Fields | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 📌 Main heading | **Section Title** (required) | *Contact Information* |
| 🗂️ Card descriptions | **Social Media / Email / Phone-WhatsApp Card Description** | A short sentence on each contact card |
| 📮 Red section (map & form) | **Badge Kecil Section** (small section badge), **Red Section Title**, **Red Section Description** | *Get In Touch*, an invitation to visit the office |
| 🏢 Office | **Office / Secretariat Name**, **Full Office Address**, **Office Hours** | *BPI Secretariat*, the full address, *Mon – Fri: 09:00 – 17:00 WIB* |

When you are done, click **Update Contact**.

## Other texts on the Contact page

Card labels, button texts ("Hubungi via WhatsApp", "Salin Alamat Email"), and the labels and messages of the **contact form** ("Nama Lengkap", "Kirim Pesan", the success message) are changed with the **Page Texts** button. See the [Page Texts](bab:teks-halaman) chapter.

Messages that visitors send through the contact form are read in the **Contact Messages** menu. The **View Incoming Messages** button on this page also opens that menu. See the [Contact Messages](bab:pesan-kontak) chapter.
