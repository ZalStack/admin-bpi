The **Profile** menu is used to change your own name, email, and password.

Open it: **Profile** in the **Account** section of the sidebar, or click your initials in the top-right corner and choose **Profile**.

## Changing your name and email

1. In **Profile Information**, change **Full Name** or **Email**.
2. Click **Save Changes**.

> **Important:** This email is used to **log in**. If you change it, use the new email next time you log in.

## Changing your password

1. In **Change Password**, fill in:
   - **Current Password**: the password you use now.
   - **New Password**: the new password, at least 8 characters.
   - **Confirm Password**: type the new password again.
2. Watch the **Password Strength** indicator. Try to get it to green.
3. Click **Update Password**.

**A strong password:**

- Is at least 12 characters long.
- Mixes upper case letters, lower case letters, numbers, and symbols.
- Does not use names, birthdays, or easy-to-guess words (such as `bpi2026` or `password123`).
- Is different from your email or social media passwords.

> **Tip:** Use a short phrase you can remember, for example `Film-Indonesia-Juara!7`, or keep your passwords in a password manager.

## Deleting your own account (Delete Account)

At the bottom of the Profile page there is a red **Delete Account** box for permanently deleting your own account.

1. Click **Delete Account**.
2. Type your **password** in **Enter your password to confirm**.
3. Click **Permanently Delete**, then click **OK** in the confirmation box.

You are then logged out and **can no longer log in** with that account. News or other content you created is **not** deleted. Click **Cancel** if you change your mind.

> **Warning:** Deleting an account **cannot be undone**. If you are only changing jobs or need a different role, **do not** delete your own account. Contact the Super Admin, who can change your role or delete your account through **Role Management**.

> **Note:** **Super Admin** accounts are protected and cannot be deleted from the Profile page.

## Using the CMS safely

- **Do not share your account** with anyone. Ask the Super Admin to create a separate account.
- **Log out** whenever you finish, especially on shared office computers.
- Do not tick **Remember Me** on public computers.
- Change your password regularly, for example every 6 months, and **right away** if you suspect someone else knows it.
- Be wary of emails or messages asking for your password. The BPI team will **never** ask for your password by email, phone, or chat.
