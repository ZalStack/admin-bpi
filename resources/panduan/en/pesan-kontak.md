The **Contact Messages** menu holds all **messages sent by visitors** through the form on the website's Contact page.

Open menu: [Contact Messages](cms:admin.kontak-form.index)

## Where do these messages come from?

1. A visitor fills in the form on the website's Contact page (name, email, subject, and message) and presses "Kirim Pesan" (send message).
2. The message is saved in **Contact Messages** with the status **Unread**.
3. At the same time, the system sends a **notification email** to the official BPI email set up by the technical team. The subject of that email starts with **[New Contact Message]**, followed by the message subject and the sender's name.

> **Note:** If the notification email never arrives (also check the Spam folder), the message is still safely stored in this menu. Report it to the technical team so they can check the email settings.

## Reading a message

The list page shows **Name**, **Email**, **Subject**, **Status** (*Read* / *Unread*), and **Date** (the date and time the message arrived). The newest messages are at the top.

1. Click the 👁️ (**Detail**) icon on a message.
2. The detail page shows the full message, the sender's details, and when it was received.

> **Important:** Opening a message does **not** change its status to *Read* automatically. After reading, click **Mark as Read** so colleagues know it has been handled.

## Replying to a message

The CMS does not send replies itself. Reply through the office email:

1. On the message detail page, click the sender's **email address**. Your computer's email app opens. You can also copy the address into Gmail/Outlook.
2. Write the reply from the **official BPI email** and send it.
3. Return to the CMS and click **Mark as Read**.

> **Tip:** If the notification email arrives in the official BPI inbox, you can simply press **Reply** on that notification email. The reply automatically goes to the person who sent the message.

## Other buttons on the detail page

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mark as Read** | Changes the status to *Read* |
| **Mark as Unread** | Changes the status back to *Unread*, for example so a colleague follows it up |
| **Delete Message** | Deletes the message permanently |

## Suggested workflow

- Check this menu **every working day**. *Unread* messages have a yellow label.
- Choose **one person in charge** so messages are not answered twice.
- Try to reply within **1–2 working days**.
- Delete **spam** (ads or meaningless messages). Important messages are best **kept** as an archive.

> **Warning:** Messages contain visitors' **personal data** (name, email, message). Do not share the content or screenshots with anyone who does not need them.
